Naan Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons of ghee or butter
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of instant yeast
  • 2 tablespoons of milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 3/4 cups of flour
  • 1 egg

Equipment

  • Small bowl
  • Large bowl
  • Large tray
  • Rolling pin
  • Cast iron skillet

Directions

  1. Mix yeast with warm water and sugar.
  2. Whisk milk and egg together.
  3. Sift flour and salt into a bowl.
  4. Combine the mixtures into dough.
  5. Let the dough rise.
  6. Roll into rounds and cook in a skillet.
  7. Brush with melted butter or ghee.
